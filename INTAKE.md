# Intake — "What are you expecting?"

Answer these 5 questions about your video. Match the answers to the
recommendation matrix below (or use the interactive chooser in
`docs/chooser.html`).

## The 5 questions

**Q1. What's the content?**
a) Education / explainer (teaching a concept)
b) Product or app promo (selling something)
c) Tutorial / process ("watch me do this")
d) Money / finance topic
e) Motivational / quote / portfolio piece

**Q2. Who's watching?**
a) Students / learners
b) Potential customers
c) Creators / editors / peers
d) General audience, casual scroll

**Q3. What's the mood?**
a) Calm, premium, trustworthy
b) Hype, maximal, fast
c) Playful, fun
d) Serious, premium

**Q4. Speaker or no speaker?**
a) Talking head on camera
b) No speaker — graphics only

**Q5. Subtitle need?**
a) Full captions synced to speech
b) Keyword emphasis only (chips/pops)
c) None — graphics carry it

---

## Recommendation matrix

| If you answered… | Pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Q1=a, Q3=a | **Warm Editorial** | calm authority + diagram acts = teaching that feels premium |
| Q1=a, Q2=a, Q3=c | **Warm Ed-tech** | friendly teacher energy, app-card friendly |
| Q1=b (app/SaaS), Q3=b | **Neon Product Ad** | built for selling through contrast |
| Q1=b, Q3=d | **Warm Editorial** or **Dark Fintech** | premium product story without hype |
| Q1=c, Q3=b/c | **Dark Sticker Collage** | the viral tutorial look, panels over talking head |
| Q1=d | **Dark Fintech** | money reads serious in green-glow dark |
| Q1=e or Q4=b (no speaker) | **Mono Kinetic Type** | type IS the content, no speaker needed |
| Q4=a + Q5=a (full captions) | **Warm Editorial** (chips) or **Warm Ed-tech** (dark pills) | both have complete caption systems |
| Q4=a + Q5=b (keywords only) | **Dark Sticker Collage** (pops) or **Warm Editorial** (chip emphasis rhythm) | emphasis-only systems |
| Q5=c (no subs) | **Neon Product Ad** or **Mono Kinetic Type** | graphics carry the narration |

## Mixes (when one style isn't enough)

- **Warm Editorial base + Neon contrast chips** — a premium explainer
  that still sells ("old way vs new way" beat).
- **Dark Sticker Collage + Warm Ed-tech cards** — hype tutorial with
  friendly app-card explainers inside.
- **Mono Kinetic Type opener + Warm Editorial body** — type-driven
  hook, then calm teaching (common in the best reference).

## Kanglish content rule of thumb

Default to **Warm Editorial** (caption chips handle Kanglish
code-mixing cleanly) and add the **3D gold native-script keyword
overlay** (see `RECIPES.md` → Kanglish hook device) on the hook beat.
For hype topics, swap the base to **Dark Sticker Collage**.
