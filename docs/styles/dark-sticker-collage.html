Motion grammar

  • Rapid panel pop-ins over a continuous talking head — one panel = one idea.
  • Panels enter with glow, sit ~3–5s, cut hard. No dissolves.
  • Emphasis words pop beside the speaker (2–4 words max), in on beat, out hard.
  • Talking head stays center-left; graphics live in the freed space.

Layout rules

  • Floating 9:16 demo cards centered over the 16:9 frame.
  • Cyan uppercase capability labels pinned around cards (CLIP / CAPTIONS / ANIMATIONS).
  • Contrast rule: B&W cutouts sit on light cards (#e7e6e1), never on the dark bg.
  • Paper-cutout hands / torn portraits / thought bubbles for “doubt” beats.

Why use it

The 2026 viral talking-head editing look. One panel = one idea keeps the energy high without chaos — the collage is the content.

Best for: Tutorials, AI-tool demos, “editing style” content, hype explainers
