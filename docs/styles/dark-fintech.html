Caption / subtitle system

Cards carry full sentences — no word captions needed. The screen-record beat doubles as a credibility device.

Motion grammar

  • Beat structure: hook cards (0–10s) → screen-record of the prompt doc (12–20s) → payoff cards + glowing “Result” (24–32s) → comment-screenshot CTA.
  • Screen-record plays mid-reel — browser tabs visible is fine; it’s content, not a watermark.
  • Smooth panel entrances; glowing script payoff is the climax.

Layout rules

  • Speaker in rounded-arch portal (radius ~120px, top y≈1024, x 90–990).
  • Card zone y≈540–945 (top third).
  • Green radial glow at bottom of canvas; faint spotlight beams.

Why use it

The money-topic dark mode. Glass + green glow reads premium-financial instantly — and showing the actual prompt/doc as a beat is a credibility device most fintech content skips.

Best for: Finance explainers, fintech products, “how X makes money” content
